- rune-kit: `SplitView`'s main pane (and `Page`'s) keeps its 1 point border whole beside content that fills it. The border used to take no layout room, so a full-bleed child with a fill (a diff, a table) painted over it along the right and bottom edges. It now takes layout room (`El::border_layout`), the content clips to its inner edge at the inner radius, as a CSS border on an `overflow: hidden` box does, and the content box is 1 point in from each edge. With `toolbar_in_panel` the sidebar's title band and toggle move down with the toolbar, so they still line up.
